# Manual validation

Use the rebuilt local app. Generated previews do not establish physical seated-view alignment.

- [ ] Select Use neutral baseline, then confirm the generated preview at open, mid, and nearly closed angles has no black gap or color wash.
- [ ] Enable live mode with Screen Recording access. If permission is missing, allow it in macOS and relaunch. Do not bypass consent.
- [ ] Keep another app and OpenLid settings visible; ensure both remain in the captured picture and the overlay never recursively captures itself.
- [ ] Lower the lid gently from your normal seated position. Tune Perspective and Clear angle; judge whether the picture remains perceptually upright rather than moving downward.
- [ ] Reopen quickly and slowly; check no jitter, overshoot, stale frame, or black flash.
- [ ] Compare Paper/Dusk/Mist, and zero/max Perspective, Softness, and Shadow.
- [ ] Pause from the menu bar while another app has focus; check capture stops and normal interaction remains possible.
- [ ] Verify rapid enable/pause, sleep/wake, lock/unlock, display changes, and permission revocation hide the overlay and stop capture.
- [ ] Hold active for 15 seconds and confirm automatic pause.
- [ ] Check full-screen apps, Spaces, HDR, CPU, GPU, and energy usage.

Record hardware, macOS, exact build, checks performed, and failures. Review screenshots for private data before sharing.
