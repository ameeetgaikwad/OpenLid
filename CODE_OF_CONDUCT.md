# Community expectations

OpenLid welcomes contributors of all experience levels and backgrounds.

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Give newcomers room to learn; explain decisions and technical tradeoffs.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or disclose private information about others.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project. Do not spam or post unrelated promotions.
- Respect consent when sharing screenshots, recordings, or diagnostics.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces maintained by the project. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads, or restrict participation. Responses should consider severity, repetition, and the safety of affected participants.

For a public moderation concern, ask a maintainer to review the relevant thread. For a sensitive concern, request a private contact channel without posting personal details or evidence publicly. The repository's maintainers must establish that channel before inviting public participation; see [the launch checklist](docs/releasing.md).
